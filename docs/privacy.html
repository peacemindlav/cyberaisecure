Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Privacy-First Commitment

CyberAiSecure is an independent engineering publication. We believe privacy is a fundamental engineering tenet, not an afterthought. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with data brokers or advertising networks.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the bare minimum information needed to deliver our publication:

  • Newsletter Subscription: If you voluntarily subscribe to the CyberAiSecure technical newsletter, we collect your email address via our secure form. This email is strictly used to deliver new engineering breakdowns and architecture updates. You may unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
  • Aggregated Anonymous Analytics: We use privacy-preserving Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure site performance and traffic trends. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, does not store local storage identifiers, and does not track users across websites.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

CyberAiSecure does not use invasive cross-site tracking cookies. We store only your local visual theme preference (light mode vs. dark mode) in your browser’s localStorage so that your viewing preference is remembered across visits.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes industry-standard infrastructure providers:

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (static asset delivery).
  • CDN & Security: Cloudflare (edge routing, DDoS defense, SSL/TLS encryption).
  • Newsletter Delivery: Google Forms / Workspace infrastructure for secure list management.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, reach out directly to the editorial team at editorial@cyberaisecure.com.
