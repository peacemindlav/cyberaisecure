About CyberAiSecure

An independent engineering and cybersecurity publication breaking down artificial intelligence risks, cloud defense architectures, and modern application security.

Our Mission

CyberAiSecure was founded to bridge the gap between rapidly deploying machine learning models and hardening production infrastructure. As generative AI transforms enterprise technology stacks, engineering teams face novel threat vectors—from indirect prompt injection and model weight tampering to CI/CD runner poisoning and post-quantum cryptographic transitions.

We provide actionable, code-level architectural deep-dives, reproducible exploit analyses, and practical zero-trust blueprints designed for software engineers, security architects, and technical leaders.

What We Cover

  • AI & LLM Vulnerabilities: OWASP Top 10 for LLMs, prompt injection countermeasures, red-teaming, and model provenance verification.
  • Supply Chain Defense: Securing GitHub Actions CI/CD workflows, OpenSSF standards, CycloneDX AIBOMs, and package ecosystem integrity.
  • Zero-Trust Architecture: Practical identity verification, micro-segmentation, and credential-less access patterns for distributed engineering teams.
  • Post-Quantum Cryptography: Practical transition roadmaps to NIST-standardized algorithms (ML-KEM, ML-DSA) and hybrid key exchanges.
  • Continuous Threat Exposure: Replacing obsolete point-in-time vulnerability audits with continuous exposure management (CTEM).

Editorial Principles

All technical guides and security blueprints published on CyberAiSecure are reviewed for accuracy, practical implementability, and ethical security disclosure standards. We do not publish generic overviews or sponsored fluff—every piece is built with engineering rigor.
