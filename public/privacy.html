Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Privacy Commitment

CyberAiSecure is an independent engineering publication dedicated to cybersecurity, artificial intelligence vulnerabilities, and cloud systems engineering. We value reader trust and are committed to transparent, responsible handling of personal data across our website (cyberaisecure.com).

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process minimal information strictly required to operate and maintain our publication:

  • Newsletter Subscription: When you subscribe to our technical newsletter, we collect your email address via our secure form. This address is used exclusively to send technical security articles, architectural blueprints, and updates. You can unsubscribe at any time via the one-click unsubscribe link in every email.
  • Aggregated Anonymous Analytics: We collect non-identifiable technical metrics (such as page views, browser type, and referrer headers) to assess site performance and popular technical topics without profiling individual readers.
  • Direct Inquiries: If you contact our editorial team via our feedback or contact forms, we use your submitted details solely to respond to your specific query.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Cookies and local storage technologies are used on CyberAiSecure for site functionality and advertising purposes:

  • Essential Functional Preferences: We use browser localStorage to store your viewing theme preference (light mode vs. dark mode) so your selection is remembered across sessions without storing personal identifiers.
  • Third-Party & Advertising Cookies: Third-party partners and advertising networks (including Google) set cookies on your device to serve relevant advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, as described in Section 4 below.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Disclosures

In accordance with Google AdSense program policies and mandatory privacy requirements:

  • Third-Party Vendors & Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to CyberAiSecure and/or other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-Party Ad Network Opt-Out: You may also opt out of third-party vendors' and ad networks' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice page at www.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out tool at optout.networkadvertising.org. European readers can manage ad preferences via the EDAA portal at youronlinechoices.com.

5. European Economic Area (EEA), UK & California Rights

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific statutory data privacy rights under regulations such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA):

  • EEA and UK Visitors (GDPR): Where applicable, advertising consent is requested prior to deploying personalized tracking cookies. You may revoke or modify your consent preferences at any time.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information for monetary value. You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data, and to opt out of cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Data Security & Infrastructure

CyberAiSecure is deployed on secure, modern hosting infrastructure with enforced HTTPS/TLS encryption. We apply standard defensive engineering controls to protect all data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
